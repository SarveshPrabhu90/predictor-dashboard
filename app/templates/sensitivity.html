How to Interpret

  • R² → 1.0 means the model explains nearly all variance in the ground truth. For a linear system with Gaussian noise, even small samples should reach high R² quickly.
  • MAE / RMSE decrease as sample size grows — the model averages out observation noise with more data.
  • Diminishing returns — beyond a certain n, adding more samples barely improves accuracy. That's the practical minimum sample size.
  • Yield vs Purity may converge at different rates depending on coefficient magnitudes and noise-to-signal ratio.

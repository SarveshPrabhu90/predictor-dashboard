How to Interpret

  • Match score = 1.0 means the learned optimizer recommends exactly the same inputs as the explicit baseline. Scores above 0.95 indicate strong agreement.
  • Small input differences are expected — the learned model trains on noisy observations, so its coefficients are slightly different.
  • Ground truth check — even if predictions differ slightly, the real plant output at the learned optimum should still be close to the explicit optimum's output.
  • Yield difference < 0.5% is a strong indicator that the learned model is operationally equivalent to the explicit baseline.
